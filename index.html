✦ Our Craft & Heritage

The Essence of
Arabian Perfume

Arabian Perfume Lab brings together the richness of Arabian perfumery and contemporary fragrance craftsmanship. Every creation is inspired by rare ingredients, timeless traditions, and the desire to create unforgettable moments.

From the ancient agarwood forests of Cambodia to the high-altitude rose valleys of Taif, we source only the finest botanicals and resins. Each fragrance is meticulously blended in small batches and macerated for months before being bottled in Guntur, Andhra Pradesh.

✦ The Palette of Creation ✦

The Ingredients Behind the Scent

Discover the precious raw botanicals, woods, and accords that form the soul of our signature compositions.

Cambodia · Agarwood

Oud

Referred to as "Liquid Gold", our wild-harvested agarwood provides an intoxicating woody, smoky, and resinous foundation that lingers for days.

Note: Base Profile: Deep, Smoky, Animalic
Kashmir · Crocus Sativus

Saffron

Hand-picked crimson stigmas that impart a rich bittersweet leather aura, warm metallic facets, and instant regal radiance.

Note: Top / Heart Profile: Warm, Leathery, Spicy
Taif · Rosa Damascena

Taif Rose

Harvested at first light in high Saudi mountain plateaus. Distilled via copper alembics to preserve its velvet honeyed petals and crisp dew.

Note: Heart Profile: Floral, Honeyed, Noble
Oman · Resinous Accord

Amber

A sumptuous fusion of labdanum, benzoin, and Madagascar vanilla, yielding a comforting golden warmth with sensual balsamic richness.

Note: Base Profile: Balsamic, Warm, Golden
Vegan Accord · Laboratory

Silk Musk

An ethereal clean-skin scent molecule created through botanical biosynthesis. Fixes aromas gracefully while radiating an intimate cashmere glow.

Note: Base Profile: Velvet, Skin, Powdery
Mysore · Santalum Album

Sandalwood

Revered for centuries for its milky, creamy, and sacred balsamic character. Smooths sharp spices and grounds delicate blossoms into harmony.

Note: Base / Heart Profile: Creamy, Woody, Serene

✦ Meet the Founder

A Passion for
Arabian Fragrance

"Every perfume I create carries a piece of the Arabian desert — the warm oud smoke, the roses of Taif, the amber of ancient trade routes. I want every customer to find their signature scent and carry that magic with them every day."

Founder, Arabian Perfume Lab Guntur, Andhra Pradesh

Arabian Perfume Lab was born in the heart of Guntur from a lifelong passion for authentic Arabian fragrance traditions. Starting with a small collection of attars and oud oils, the boutique has grown into a full fragrance house — serving customers across all of India through our WhatsApp ordering service.

Every bottle you see on our shelves has been personally selected, tested, and approved by our founder. We offer over 200 fragrances — from classic attars to premium inspired EDPs — all available for All India Delivery.
